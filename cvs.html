TL;DR

This isn't one project. It's the studio I've been running alone since 2021, over 40 projects in branding and social media design for startups, creators and D2C brands.

My Role

What I actually did

It's a one person studio. Every project runs through me, start to finish.

I started Creative Vision Studios as a way to put my own work out into the world. There was no team behind it, just me taking on projects, learning what clients actually needed, and getting better with every brief.

Everything fed into the same goal: work that felt considered, not rushed, even when it was just one person doing it.

The work

A mix of client work, one studio.

Every client came in with a different problem, a different industry, a different budget, and no shared playbook that would work for all of them.

I moved between branding and social content depending on what each client actually needed, building systems that could flex rather than forcing every brief into the same process.

That flexibility is part of what's kept clients coming back across 40+ projects over 5+ years running the studio alone.

Reflections

What this taught me

Every client teaches you a new constraint.

Running a studio across so many categories, tech startups, creators, D2C brands, means no two briefs ever look the same. That range is what pushed me from being a designer who executes to someone who leads the whole creative direction.

Since then, that range has become the thing I bring into every project, even the ones with a single, focused problem to solve.
